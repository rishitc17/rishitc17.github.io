# Rishit Choudhary

Source: owner's portfolio brief, 29 October 2026.

Personal portfolio for an IB student who builds software, writes, explores mathematics and creates Blender work. Precise, ambitious, curious and playful. Polished editorial composition without serif-headline clichés, terminal costumes, neon blue, glass panels or decorative particles.

ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

The supplied R profile image is the favicon. Its #FD0835 red informs the accent; accessible darker red serves text on the light ground. The light ground gives work room to breathe; an equally considered dark theme supports viewer preference.

Archivo supplies architectural headlines without a literary serif costume. Manrope supplies legible, warm body text. Monospace belongs only to actual ASCII art and code.

The original chocolate-iced Blender donut is the identity motif: one object translated between material and mathematics. Realistic and ASCII representations share a render target and camera. Cursor distance changes the representation per character cell, never revealing an unrelated second object.

Oversized name typography frames the donut, while a smaller description identifies its owner. Projects use wide visual stories, writing uses an editorial list, renders use an image gallery, and recognition uses a compact archive. Empty sections say they are empty; no fictional projects or certificates.

Spacing uses 8/16/24/40/64/96px roles, with shorter phone sections. Image surfaces crop only images. Rounded controls use 6px corners; artwork surfaces stay square. Shadows are reserved for the modal elevation. Red marks the primary action and active controls, not every surface.

Motion explains the material-to-character transformation, introduces the hero once, and acknowledges interactions. Native scrolling, reduced-motion support, pause controls and offscreen suspension protect usability. No looping decorative animations or custom cursor.

The design-taste draw suggested a name-as-monument composition. Its serif type and crossfading photographs conflict with the owner's technical sans direction and synchronized model requirement; the supplied direction takes priority. No unrelated stock pictures are introduced to fill missing personal content.
